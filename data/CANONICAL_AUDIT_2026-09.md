# Canonical chronology/location audit — September 2026

Updated against the supplied TimelineTWD location/map reference, Nerdist's July 28 2026 timeline synthesis, and the Walking Dead Wiki's TV Series / Television Universe / Chronological Episode Order / series-location indexes.

## Canonical anchors applied

- Wildfire global outbreak: August 25, 2010; Monument Day: August 27, 2010.
- TWD S1–2: Oct–Nov 2010; S3: Jun–Jul 2011; S4–8: Jan–Jun 2012.
- TWD S9 E1–5: Feb–Mar 2014; E6–15: Oct 2020; E16: Jan 2021.
- TWD S10: Aug–Sep 2021.
- TWD S11 main story: Sep 2021–Apr 2022; the finale's epilogue is summer 2023.
- Fear S4 is 2012; Fear S5 is 2013; Fear S6–7 are 2014; Fear S8 is 2022.
- World Beyond S1 is 2020; S2 is 2021.
- The Ones Who Live contains flashbacks from 2014 onward, with the reunion/current storyline in autumn 2022 and the Alexandria return in summer 2023.
- Daryl Dixon S1–S3 are part of the 2023 frontier arc; S3 moves through the UK and Spain.
- Dead City S1 is 2027, S2 is 2028, and S3 continues in 2028.
- Tales is episode-specific: Evie/Joe 2011; Blair/Gina 2010; Dee 2012–2013; Amy/Dr. Everett 2020; Davon 2025; La Doña remains chronologically and geographically unknown.

## Location corrections/additions

- Added an explicit Tales-only Atlanta node so Blair/Gina can be linked without conflating series ownership.

- Alexandria moved to its real-world Alexandria, Virginia anchor.
- Hilltop moved to Culpeper, Virginia.
- Kingdom moved to Arlington County, Virginia.
- Sanctuary moved to Fairfax, Virginia.
- The Bricks and New Babylon moved from New York/New Jersey coordinates to approximate Virginia positions; they are Virginia locations in canon.
- Added World Beyond anchors for Lincoln/Nebraska/Iowa/New York/Albany/Watkins Glen/The Perimeter/CRM research.
- Added Daryl anchors for Marseille, Le Havre, Agen, London, Galicia and Seville.
- Added Dead City anchors for New York City, Brooklyn Bridge, Central Park, Statue of Liberty and Madison Square Garden.
- Added Tales anchors for Madawaska and the Amy/Dr. Everett ranger station; La Doña's house is intentionally unknown.

## Data-quality rule

The atlas should not assign an entire season's location list to every episode. Episode location IDs now use narrower anchors where the source material supports them, and unknown road/interstitial locations are left unassigned rather than fabricated.
